Who sees what · read-only shares

Merchandising agentonly the tables its share names
Store Operations agentSecure Views filter its rows
Finance agentSecure Views mask columns
Executivesevery query visible and traceable
  • Shares are read-only by design — an agent on a share cannot write
  • A share exposes only the tables you name — the agent does not see that other tables exist
  • Secure Views filter rows and hide or mask columns
  • Every query the agent writes is visible and traceable
  • Data resident in Sydney (AWS ap-southeast-2)
  • Replacing Qlik is only a future option. The implications are not known today, and nothing here commits to it.
