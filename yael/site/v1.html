How I work

Not one method. The right mix for you.

CBT

About me

Hi, I'm Yael.

I'm a certified CBT psychotherapist with a Master's degree in Arts Therapy, and a mother of three. For over twenty years I've worked with children, teenagers, adults and parents — in schools, clinics, councils and online.

People sometimes tell me, "I didn't know you also work with that." That's the point of this page: if you're struggling, there's a good chance I can help — and if I can't, I'll tell you honestly and point you to someone who can.
