How I work

My way of working.

It matters to me that you feel comfortable and can speak openly. Together we'll try to understand what's hard, what you'd like to change, and what might help. I combine approaches and tools to suit the person in front of me, with room both for emotional processing and for practical work.

The aim is for what we do in therapy to stay with you in everyday life, and to strengthen your ability to cope on your own.

1We get to know each other and understand what's on your mind and what matters to you.
2We set goals together and choose ways of working that suit you.
3We try things out, see what helps, and adjust as we go.

About me

Nice to meet you, I'm Yael.

I'm Yael Kario, a psychotherapist trained in cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), with a Master's degree in arts therapy — movement and dance.

For more than twenty years I've walked alongside children, teens, adults and parents. Over the years I've worked in educational and public settings and in private practice, in Israel and in Australia.

I've also completed EMDR training (Level 2). My work has included government bodies such as the Ministry of Defence, the Ministry of Education and local councils, and I have treated soldiers in complex emotional states, including those affected by the "Swords of Iron" war.

Since the COVID years I've developed ways of working online as well, such as remote play and drawing together online.

For me, therapy is first of all a connection between people. It matters to me to create a space of listening, honesty and respect, and to combine emotional understanding with tools you can take into everyday life.

I'm a mother of three and an Israeli living in Sydney. Life between countries and cultures is part of my story, and part of my work with people and families going through change and transition.

Relocation

Finding your place, even in a new one.

Moving to a new country can be exciting, but it can also bring homesickness, loneliness and uncertainty. Sometimes each person in the family adjusts at a different pace. As an Israeli living in Sydney, I know this from the inside. In therapy there's room for whatever the move brings up, and together we look for ways to build stability and belonging.

Therapy in my Sydney clinic or online, in English or Hebrew. Session times are arranged with you personally.
