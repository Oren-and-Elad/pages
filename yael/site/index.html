How I work

I'm not here to keep you in therapy.

I'm here to give you my knowledge and my tools — and see you move on. My clients laugh that I "fire" them. If we're going in circles, I'll tell you, and we'll change course or find you someone who fits better.

1We understand what's going on — together, in plain words.
2We choose the tools that fit you — not one method for everyone.
3You practise, you grow, you go — with tools you keep for life.

About me

Hi, I'm Yael.

A certified CBT psychotherapist with a Master's in Arts Therapy, and a mother of three. For more than twenty years I've worked with children, teens, adults and parents — in schools, clinics, councils and online, in Israel and in Australia.

I believe in connection: between people, between cultures, and between you and the help you actually need. Therapy, for me, is first of all a human meeting — a place to come as you are.
